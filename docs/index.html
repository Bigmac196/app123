Frequently asked

Does my data leave my phone?
The product analysis itself never leaves your device. The free version shows ads (Google AdMob), which is the only part that uses the network or collects data. The ad-free upgrade removes ads and ad tracking. See our Privacy Policy.

Is HypeCheck always right?
No. Verdicts are heuristic estimates based on the listing text — not lab tests, and not medical or financial advice. Use them as a sanity check.

How do I remove ads?
Tap the crown icon or “Remove ads” in the app and choose the one-time unlock or a subscription. Either one removes all ads.
