WorthIt Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 17, 2026

This policy explains what WorthIt (“the app”, “we”) does with information when you use it. We designed the app to keep your activity private by default.

1. The product analysis is on your device

When you check a product link, the app downloads that single web page directly from the retailer to your iPhone and analyzes it locally. The link you check, the page content, the verdict, and your check history are stored only on your device. We do not operate any server, account system, or database, and this information is never sent to us.

2. Advertising (free version)

The free version shows ads using Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google’s software may collect and process information such as device and advertising identifiers, IP address, general (coarse) location, and app-usage/interaction data. This is the only part of WorthIt that uses the network for anything other than fetching the product page you asked for, and the only part that collects data.

Google’s handling of this data is governed by Google’s policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

3. App Tracking Transparency

On first launch the app asks for permission to track using Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, ads still appear but are non-personalized. You can change this anytime in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

4. Removing ads (premium)

If you buy the one-time ad-free unlock or an ad-free subscription, ads and ad-related data collection stop. Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store; we do not see or store your payment information. Apple’s handling of purchases is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

5. Data we collect ourselves

None. We have no analytics, no accounts, and no servers. We never receive your checks, history, or personal information.

6. Children

WorthIt is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ads shown are subject to Google’s policies and your tracking choice.

7. Data retention & deletion

Your check history lives only on your device. Delete individual items by swiping in History, or remove everything by deleting the app.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the “Effective date” above.

9. Contact

Questions? Email support@example.com.
